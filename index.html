Why teach here

Your Swahili is the qualification. Your time is what gets paid.

You do not need a teaching certificate, a laptop or a lesson plan. If you can explain “pole pole” to someone who has never heard it, you can teach here — and get paid for the hour you spend doing it.

Paid by the hour

Every teaching session is timed and paid at your hourly rate. The earnings bar in the chat shows the minutes and shillings adding up as you go.

Learners are already here

No marketing and no chasing students. 181 foreigners have already signed up, chosen their level and written down why they want to learn.

No lesson planning

Chats open with a greeting and suggested phrases. Teach the basics you already use every day — greetings, numbers, prices, directions.

Teach from your phone

Between lectures, on a lunch break, in a matatu queue. The whole teaching workspace is thumb-sized and works on any screen.

Bilingual by design

Every Swahili message carries a short English gloss, so a beginner follows the thread without you repeating yourself three times.

You set the pace

Accept a session when you are free, step away when you are not. No schedules, no penalties and no minimum hours to keep your place.

How it works

From opening the app to your first paid session in under a minute.

The process is deliberately simple. No sign-up wall, no interview, no placement test — and nothing to install.

1

Browse the learners

Open the chat list and scan 181 foreigners from 20 countries. Each one shows their level, country and why they want to learn Swahili.

2

Pick a chat

Choose a learner whose level and goal you can support. Search by name or country if you already have someone in mind.

3

Say Jambo

Every conversation opens with a friendly greeting from the learner, so there is never an awkward first message to think up.

4

Teach and earn

Reply at your own pace. The session timer runs, the earnings add up, and your balance grows with every minute you teach.

Inside the session

Teaching that feels like chatting — and pays like work.

The session screen does the quiet work: it greets the learner, translates for them, and keeps a running total of your time. You bring the language and the patience.

  • Instant greeting: the learner opens with a Jambo and a short introduction, so you never start cold.
  • Built-in translation: each Swahili line carries a short English gloss, so beginners keep up with you.
  • Suggested replies: helpful phrases appear after the greeting — tap one and keep teaching.
  • Session timer & earnings: every minute is logged and added to your balance at your hourly rate.
